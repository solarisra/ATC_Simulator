This is a proposed one-way confidentiality agreement for adult, individual
testers. It accompanies the [closed-test EULA](EULA.md). Complete all placeholders

The proposed seven-year period runs from each disclosure, not simply from the
signature date. Review whether that duration is reasonable for each category of

should assess trade-secret measures and any statutory notice requirements,
including 18 U.S.C. Section 1833(b) if the relationship falls within its scope.

is included. This document does not itself obtain assent or restrict anyone

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means nonpublic information disclosed by Discloser to
Tester on or after the effective date for the Purpose that is marked confidential
or that a reasonable person would understand is confidential in this closed test,
including:

- Closed test builds, nonpublic source code, private repository content, and
  unreleased assets or technical documentation.
- Nonpublic features, defects, performance results, and behavior revealed by
  operating the closed test build.
- Screenshots, recordings, streams, transcripts, or extracts revealing that
  information.
- Private roadmap, security, access, and test-coordination information.
- Test reports or feedback to the extent they reveal the foregoing.

An unannounced feature is not authorized for disclosure merely because a
different feature or a public demo has been announced.

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

- Is or becomes publicly available without breach of a duty by Tester.
- Tester lawfully knew without confidentiality restrictions before disclosure.
- Tester independently develops without using Confidential Information.
- Tester lawfully receives from a third party without a confidentiality duty.
- Discloser expressly releases in writing for the specific intended disclosure.

Public third-party code and data do not become confidential merely because they
are included in a test build. Nonpublic integrations or modifications may still
qualify. Public release removes confidentiality only for information actually
made public, not unrelated source code, defects, credentials, or private plans.

## 4. Use and protection duties

Tester must use Confidential Information only for the Purpose and protect it
with reasonable care, no less than the care used for comparable personal
confidential information.

Without Discloser's prior written approval covering the proposed disclosure,
Tester must not:

- Share builds, source, access links, passwords, or Confidential Information with
  friends, other testers, organizations, or the public.
- Publish screenshots, gameplay recordings, livestreams, nonpublic test results,
  or descriptions revealing Confidential Information.
- Upload Confidential Information to public repositories, forums, file-sharing
  sites, or third-party AI/analysis services.

Use Discloser's designated private reporting channels. Permit access only as
authorized in writing; an invitation to one tester does not authorize household,
classroom, or team access.

Ordinary private device backups may be made under the same safeguards and may
not be used to distribute the build.

## 5. Lawful and protected disclosures

Nothing prohibits reporting suspected unlawful conduct to government agencies,
communicating with legal counsel, participating in a protected investigation,
or making disclosures protected by whistleblower or other nonwaivable law.
No prior approval or notice is required where prohibited by law.

Where applicable, an individual is not liable under federal or state trade-secret
law for disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a federal, state, or
local government official, directly or indirectly, or to an attorney, solely to
report or investigate a suspected violation of law; or in a complaint or other
document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding. An individual suing
for retaliation for reporting a suspected violation may disclose the trade
secret to the individual's attorney and use it in the proceeding if documents
containing it are filed under seal and it is not otherwise disclosed except by
court order.

For other legally compelled disclosure, provide prompt notice if legally
permitted, disclose only what is required, and reasonably cooperate with a
protective-order request at Discloser's expense. This section does not require
Tester to violate law or a binding order.

## 6. Duration

This Agreement covers disclosures made while participation is authorized.
The confidentiality and use restrictions for each item continue for **seven
(7) years from its disclosure to Tester**, unless that item earlier qualifies
for an exclusion under Section 3.

Leaving the test or termination of access does not restart or shorten that
period. Identical redisclosure alone does not restart it; materially new
confidential information has its own disclosure date.

This contractual period does not determine the duration of copyright or other
independently applicable statutory rights.

## 7. Suspected leak, return, and deletion

If Tester discovers loss, unauthorized access, or disclosure, Tester must notify
Discloser without unreasonable delay, describe the known facts, and take
reasonable steps within Tester's control to stop further disclosure. Do not
destroy evidence or violate preservation obligations.

Upon request or the end of participation, stop use and return or delete
Confidential Information and test copies, and reasonably confirm completion in
writing. Tester may retain material legally required to be preserved or
reasonably needed by legal counsel for legal compliance or a dispute. Copies in
routine backups that cannot reasonably be individually deleted may remain until
ordinary secure deletion, without restoration or use except for lawful
preservation or recovery followed by renewed deletion where required.

Retained material remains subject to this Agreement for its applicable term.
No remote wipe, unrestricted device inspection, or disclosure of unrelated
personal information is authorized.

## 8. Ownership, feedback, and no work obligation

No ownership or implied license in Confidential Information is transferred.
The EULA addresses voluntary feedback permissions. This Agreement does not
require work, assign unrelated inventions, create employment, or promise
compensation or a future commercial game license.

applicable law. Any claim remains subject to required proof, causation,

**No fixed payment, liquidated damages amount, per-download charge, or automatic

Discloser may seek temporary or permanent injunctive relief where available.

Attorney's fees and costs are recoverable only if and to the extent a court
awards them under applicable law. This Agreement creates no independent
